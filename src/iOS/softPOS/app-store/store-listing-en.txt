## What's New in This Version (version 1.1)

Beamio POS now loads the merchant terminal at pos.beamio.app — Check Balance, Top-up, and Charge in one native app.

• Opens pos.beamio.app for the full Beamio POS experience on iPhone and iPad
• Native NFC prompt when waiting for a customer card (Check Balance and Top-up)
• Native QR scanner if NFC is cancelled or unavailable
• Print receipt from success screens via AirPrint
• Universal Links support for pos.beamio.app
• Stability and WebView performance improvements

Thank you for using Beamio POS.
