## Description (max 4000 characters)

Beamio is a smart loyalty and stored-value wallet built for modern local commerce. Our pilot launches at Vancouver's Old Alley—we've rebuilt traditional loyalty with secure, seamless technology that keeps every visit simple and your assets under your control.

KEY FEATURES

Static QR for Easy Top-ups
When you reload at the counter, show your unique static identity code. The merchant scans it and your balance updates right away.

Dynamic QR for Secure Payments
Security is our priority. Powered by Smart Account technology, Beamio creates a time-sensitive, one-time dynamic QR code for each checkout—helping prevent screenshot fraud and protect your stored value.

Unified Asset Dashboard
Ditch the cluttered physical wallet. View balances, vouchers, and membership tiers across participating local merchants in one unified interface.

Physical Card Integration
Prefer a physical touch? Link a Beamio Generic NFC Pass to your account. Use QR on your phone or tap with your card—balances stay in sync in real time.

Privacy First
Your transaction history and personal data stay between you and the merchant you choose to shop with.

OPEN BEAMIO LINKS IN THE APP
Shared redeem and program links from beamio.app open directly in the app when installed—no extra browser tab required.

WHO IT'S FOR

• Beamio members and local shoppers
• Anyone using stored value, vouchers, or merchant loyalty programs
• Users who want a home-screen shortcut to their Beamio wallet on iPhone

REQUIREMENTS

• Internet connection
• NFC optional — required only when linking a physical NFC pass
• Camera optional — used only when a flow in the app requires scanning

Beamio is a consumer wallet for account holders. For in-store merchant checkout, businesses use the separate Beamio SoftPOS app.

Questions? Contact Beamio support through beamio.app.
